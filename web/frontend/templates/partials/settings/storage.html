{# Storage: where sessions are written and what is kept. Audio retention is off by default and stated plainly, because a recording of a room full of people is a different thing from a transcript of it. #}

Location

A path on the machine running this application. One SQLite file per session, written as the talk proceeds rather than at the end — so a crash costs seconds, not the session.

s

Retention

Off by default, deliberately. A recording of a room is a materially different thing to hold than a transcript of it, and it is also large — roughly 115 MB an hour.

days

Leave empty to keep everything.
