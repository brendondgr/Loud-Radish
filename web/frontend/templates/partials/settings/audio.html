1× is real time and behaves exactly like a live source. Higher speeds reprocess a long recording quickly — useful for testing, but the pipeline will start dropping audio if it cannot keep up.

Input level

Start recording to see the level. Speech should reach the middle without pinning to the end.

Speech detection

Silence is never sent to the model. This both saves compute and prevents the model inventing words from nothing, which is its most common failure on a quiet room.

Higher catches quieter speech at the cost of letting more room noise through. If whole sentences are going missing, raise it.

Preprocessing

Removes air conditioning, projector fans, and desk knocks below the cut-off. On by default; it removes nothing a speaker produces.

Hz

Helps with a speaker who moves away from the microphone. Leave it off for a lapel mic, where it mostly amplifies room noise between sentences.

ms

How much audio is handled at once. Smaller reacts faster and costs more; 32 ms suits almost everything.
