{# The post-recording export window (D-037). Reported: "On recording stop, I want a dedicated window rather than the current minimal flow" — one that previews the recording, offers the video and audio options, shows the projected size for each before committing, and then shows the pipeline running through its stages. Two halves in one dialog, swapped rather than stacked. **Choosing** is a preview, a list of presets each carrying what it would produce for *this* recording, and a size that moves as the choice does. **Watching** is one row per stage, each with its own bar and its own estimate. They are the same window because they are the same task, and because the thing you want to see when an export finishes is what it produced against what it promised. Reuses the modal shell and the focus trap, as the pre-flight sheet does. Focus containment and restoration are the accessibility behaviour most easily got wrong twice; there is one implementation of it and this is the third caller. The sizes are **ranges** and say so. A constant-quality encode depends on what is on screen, and this window's whole purpose is deciding whether a file is small enough to send — a figure presented as exact would be wrong in precisely the way that matters. #}
