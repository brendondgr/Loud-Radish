{# The pre-flight options sheet (D-020). The three window-capture options are **per-run**, not settings. A user who recorded one window without video should not silently get no video the next time, so the choice is made explicitly each run — and this is the only place it can be made, because capture has not started yet. Reuses the modal shell and the focus trap rather than introducing a second dialog primitive: focus containment and restoration are the accessibility behaviour most easily got wrong twice. The window itself is **not** chosen here. Under Wayland an application cannot enumerate windows; the desktop's own picker opens after this sheet is confirmed, which is why the confirm button says what happens next rather than "Start". #}

Which audio to record
{# The single most important sentence in this dialog. The screen-cast portal carries video only, so a window's sound has to be captured separately — and until this was fixed the separate capture was the *microphone*, so recording a talk also recorded the person watching it. It now reads the machine's audio output, which contains no microphone at all. A user who assumes otherwise and records the wrong source has lost the recording. #}
