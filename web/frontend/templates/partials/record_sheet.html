{# The record sheet (D-075). Every recording starts here. It replaces two things at once: the header's Live / Recorded / Window tabs, and the pre-flight sheet only Window used to open. The tabs named implementations rather than choices, so the sheet asks the two questions they had tangled together — *what am I recording* and *when should it be transcribed* — and the server derives the mode from the answers. Sections appear in order and only when they apply: the microphone list for a microphone, the window's audio and the video switch for a window. That reads as stepping through without Back and Next buttons, and without a second dialog primitive. Reuses the modal shell and the focus trap: focus containment and restoration are the accessibility behaviour most easily got wrong twice. The window itself is **not** chosen here. Under Wayland an application cannot enumerate windows; the desktop's own picker opens after this sheet is confirmed, which is why the confirm button says what happens next rather than "Start" whenever a window is chosen. #}
