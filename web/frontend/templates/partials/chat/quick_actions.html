{# One-tap prompts, in a pop-up beside the box (redesign Step 8). The questions a listener actually has are predictable — what did I miss, what was that term, what is the argument — and typing them out during a talk costs attention the talk needs. Each one is a configured prompt, so the set is editable rather than baked in. They were a row of chips that took a third of the pane's height; the owner chose a menu behind an icon. Filled by `chat-pane.js`, one `menuitem` per prompt. #}
