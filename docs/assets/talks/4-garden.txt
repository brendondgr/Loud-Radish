The city has cut our allowance by a fifth this summer, so I am proposing drip lines on every bed and no more hand watering with open hoses. Two of the four bays are falling apart, and the volunteers asked for a weekend workshop to rebuild them with reclaimed pallets. Third, plot assignments. We have eleven people on the waiting list and two plots that have not been touched since April. I suggest we send those two plot holders a friendly reminder, give them until the end of the month, and then offer the plots to the waiting list in order. Let's vote on each one.
